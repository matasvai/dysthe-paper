accuracy. Separate training and inference costs.
\section{Interpretation and reproducibility}
Connect each claim to its run and figure. Distinguish computational improvement
from wave-physics discovery. Do not present trends as theorems or infer
long-time laws from unresolved runs.
Freeze code revisions; record configurations, data and checkpoint hashes,
artifact locations and software versions. Use PDF figures and retain compact
numerical figure data. The claim ledger is initially empty.
